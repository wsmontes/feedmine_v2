NOTICE SURFACE — what the reader actually saw

Measured on 2026-10-20, simulator "FeedMine V1V2 Upgrade" (B89B80FE-6B5D-423E-B1F4-519F1AB0737F),
three launches at 11:28, 11:31 and 11:33.

WHAT HAPPENED
- The first V2 launch wrote the receipt with status=completed and notice_presented=0.
- The alert titled "Da versão anterior" was never visible: the accessibility tree of the running
  Simulator window was polled every 0.2 s for 15 s on two launches (and once for 12 s) and never
  contained the alert, and simctl screenshots at t≈4 s / t≈7 s show the reader's empty state, not an
  alert.
- The alert was nevertheless dismissed without the reader seeing it: notice_presented was reset to 0
  three times in reader_legacy_upgrade and flipped back to 1 on every launch before the first screen
  was readable. The only writer is AppComposition.acknowledgeLegacyUpgradeNotice()
  (AppComposition.swift:1169-1174), reached from the SwiftUI alert binding's set(false)
  (FeedMineApp.swift:324-332): the alert is presented and torn down in the same launch, so the reader
  never gets to read it.

=> In this build, on this simulator, the D7-A notice is consumed without being presented.
   The receipt keeps the counts; the copy never reaches the screen.

Those three strings are absent from the built app's en.lproj and pt-BR.lproj Localizable.strings
(checked with `plutil -p` against FeedMine.app/*.lproj/Localizable.strings), so String(localized:)
returns each key verbatim: the copy is identical in every locale, which is also why the Portuguese
copy appeared inside an otherwise English UI.

NOT CLAIMED

the measurement that establishes it.
